From invoices to inventory, from POS to payroll — Suwin Accounting covers your entire operation in one place.

Smart Invoicing

Create, send, and track professional invoices in seconds. Proforma, sales, and custom invoices with automated numbering.

Inventory Management

Real-time stock tracking, multi-location transfers, manufacturing production, and low-stock alerts built in.

Point of Sale

Full-featured POS with customer display, loyalty points, receipts, and seamless end-of-day cashier reconciliation.

Financial Reports

Balance sheet, profit & loss, trial balance, cash flow, VAT report — all generated in real time from your live data.

Banking & Payments

Bank reconciliation, cheque printing, payment vouchers, and full payment reversal workflows with audit trails.

2FA, role-based access control, encrypted data at rest, and an approval workflow that keeps sensitive transactions safe.
