Day Summary — Columns

Day Summary

One row per column of the monthly report, left to right in Order. Each column takes its figures from Report lines (the day's GL entries), Daily Sales rows (amount, or NOS for room counts), or is typed in per day on the Day Summary tab. A TOTAL adds the ticked In total columns since the previous TOTAL; TOTAL (everything) adds them all. Less takes another column off this one (e.g. BAR SALE less KEG while keg sales are still posted in bar sales). Hold Ctrl to pick several items.
