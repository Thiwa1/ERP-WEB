Reversals & Adjustments
Cheques dated ahead entered on Bank Payment or Customer Receipt land here first — they don't hit the books until you Clear them. Clear posts the invoice settlement, journal entry and bank record as of the post date. Reschedule changes the post date (e.g. a bounced cheque replaced with a new one). Cancel drops it with no GL effect, since it was never posted.
